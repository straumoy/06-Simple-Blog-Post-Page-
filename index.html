Why I started Learning Web Development

By Ole Straumoy, November 13, 2025

Photo by Markus Spiske from Pexels.com

Like many beginners, I was always facinated by how websites work. In 2025, I finally decided to dive into the workd of web development and start building things on my own.

I began with HMTL, the basic structure of the web. It was exciting to see how simple tags could create headings, paragraphs, images, and links.

After going through w3schools excelent tutorials (and bragging about on LinkedIn at every oppertunity), I'm cutting my teeth at djamware's 25 basic HTML projects.

This is just the begining of my journey, and I'm looking forward to sharing what I learn along the way!
